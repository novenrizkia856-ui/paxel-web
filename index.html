The problem

Tokens move. Their story gets lost.

A token proves ownership. It rarely proves what the asset is.

  1. 01

    Scattered records

    Asset data lives in silos. Nobody sees the full picture.

  2. 02

    Fragile trust

    Buyers rely on screenshots and promises. Proof stays out of reach.

  3. 03

    No lasting memory

    Platforms close. Records vanish. The asset loses its past.

The value

One record. Always with the asset.

Identhify gives every tokenized asset a single source of truth.

  • Permanent

    A passport outlives every platform it touches.

  • Verifiable

    Anyone can check a claim against its source.

  • Portable

    The record travels wherever the token goes.

  • Neutral

    No trading. No custody. Only identity and history.

  • Ordered

    Every event is written in sequence. Nothing is overwritten.

  • Open

    A public layer for the whole ecosystem.

How it works

Four steps. One lasting record.

A simple lifecycle. Designed to stay true for decades.

  1. 01

    Issue

    An asset receives its passport. Its identity is fixed.

  2. 02

    Attest

    Trusted parties add signed facts. Audits, appraisals, inspections.

  3. 03

    Record

    Every change becomes an event. The history only grows.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Anyone can read the passport. Trust comes from proof.
